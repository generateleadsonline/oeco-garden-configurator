Privacy for this design preview

This demonstration configurator is operated by Generate Leads Online (GLO). It is a design concept for Oeco Garden Rooms and is not connected to Oeco’s sales inbox or CRM.

What we save and why

When you submit the form, GLO saves your name, email, installation postcode, optional phone number, intended timing, room choices and generated preview. We record your acknowledgement of this notice and the date of submission. This lets us save your design, calculate an estimate and generate your PDF pack. No email is automatically sent to you or Oeco and you are not added to a marketing list.

Where the details are held

Submitted designs and contact details are stored in a private Netlify Blobs store belonging to this GLO demonstration project. The estimate and PDF are accessed using a secure, HttpOnly session cookie valid for seven days. The PDF is generated on request and is not placed at a public download URL. Netlify processes hosting data under its own privacy terms.

Your device

Your room selections are saved in local storage on your device so you can continue your design. Contact details are not stored in local storage. The session cookie is used only to retrieve your saved estimate and design pack. Design links contain room choices, never contact information or prices. We do not install advertising trackers in this preview.

Your choices

You can explore and share a design without submitting contact details. Clear your browser’s site data to remove local choices and the session cookie. For access, correction or removal of a submitted preview enquiry, contact the GLO operator through Generate Leads Online and quote your design reference. Preview records are retained for evaluation until the operator removes them; the seven-day cookie expiry does not delete the server record.

If you choose an Oeco website, telephone or email link, you will contact Oeco directly under Oeco’s privacy notice. We do not transfer this form submission to them.
